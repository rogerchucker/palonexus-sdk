# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the PaloNexus community free from
harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and attentive to the effect of your words and
actions. Welcome questions, give actionable feedback, respect boundaries, and
focus disagreement on ideas. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, and publication of private information are
unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through this repository's private reporting
form at
<https://github.com/rogerchucker/palonexus-sdk/security/advisories/new> and
identify the report as a Code of Conduct matter. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and confidentially.

If a report involves the repository owner, or that private form is unsuitable,
contact GitHub Support at <https://support.github.com/contact> and identify the
repository and conduct concern. Do not send an owner-involved report to the
repository owner.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily ban, or
permanently ban participants whose conduct is inappropriate. Retaliation
against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents
the project.
